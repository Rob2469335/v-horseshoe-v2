## Core Design Overview:
- Uses a dictionary (`self.cache`) mapping keys to linked nodes.
- Nodes store key, value, pointers (prev/next), and handle movement within [K
the doubly-linked list during access or update operations.
- The head always points to the least recently used item; tail is most rece[4D[K
